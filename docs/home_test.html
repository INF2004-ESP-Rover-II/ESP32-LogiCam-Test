Two-marker home test

iPhone (Continuity Camera) on a stand, the two printed 77 mm markers standing up on a table. Move the markers, keep the phone still, and keep the terminal visible to read the results.

Setup (top view)

python3 laptop/vision_publisher.py --marker-mm 77 --source 1 --calib laptop/camera_calib_iphone.json --record laptop/home_2markers.jsonl

Checks, in order (camera's view unless noted)

a

Distance check

One marker 50 cm from the lens, measured with a tape.

≈ 500 mm ±20≈ 0 deg
b

Bearing sign

Slide the marker to the right, then to the left.

right + deg left − deg
c

Both markers

Both markers in view at once.

2 IDs on one lineright types
d

Hold still

Hold one marker still for about 5 seconds.

mm changes by a few only
e

Quick block

Pass your hand over the marker quickly.

no_marker→ok
f

Leave and return

Take the marker out of view for 3 s, then bring it back.

same ID returns(a "revisit" for B1)
g

Bad view

Turn the marker steeply away, or move it far back.

INVALID:too_smallhigh_reproj_erroror it disappears
h

Cover the lens

Cover the iPhone camera with your finger.

no_markercamera fine, sees nothing
i

Fake a stall

Press f in the video window, wait 6 s, press f again.

stalled→disconnected→ok

Then replay it

python3 laptop/replay.py laptop/home_2markers.jsonl | python3 laptop/receiver_example.py

The receiver should print the same story: both IDs, the gaps and the health changes.

Send back

  • The calibration RMS (good: under 0.5 px)
  • The two marker IDs
  • The reading for check a
  • Any check that did not match
